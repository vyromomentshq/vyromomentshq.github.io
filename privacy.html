Privacy Policy — VyroMoments

Last updated: October 24, 2026

VyroMoments ("we", "us") operates the VyroMoments Facebook Page, the @vyromoments Instagram account, and this website. This policy explains what information our Meta developer application (the "App") accesses and how it is used.

What the App accesses

The App is used only by VyroMoments' own operator to publish VyroMoments' own content to VyroMoments' own Facebook Page and Instagram account. To do that, the App accesses:

  • The name and numeric ID of our own Facebook Page.
  • The numeric ID and username of our own linked Instagram account.
  • An access token for our own Page, generated by our own operator.
  • Basic engagement data for our own posts (views, likes, comments), used to understand how our content performs.

The App does not access the private data of other Facebook or Instagram users, does not read anyone's messages, and does not collect information about visitors to this website.

How the information is used

  • Publishing our own videos and posts to our own Page and account.
  • Reviewing the performance of our own content.

We never sell personal information. We never share access tokens or account data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored in encrypted secure storage, are never published, and are used only for the purposes above. Tokens are deleted or revoked when they are no longer needed, and can be revoked at any time from the Facebook account's Settings → Business Integrations.

Your choices and data deletion

If you believe the App has accessed any information relating to you and you would like it deleted, contact us by sending a message to the VyroMoments Facebook Page. We will respond and delete any such data within 30 days. Because the App only operates on VyroMoments' own accounts, it does not hold data about other users in the ordinary course of operation.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
